Sales performance analysis using Power BI.

 Project Overview

This project analyzes sales performance using Microsoft Power BI. The goal is to turn sales data into useful insights that support business decision-making.

Objectives

* Analyze overall sales performance.
* Identify trends and patterns in the data.
* Compare performance across relevant categories.
* Present key findings through an interactive dashboard.

Tools Used

* Microsoft Power BI
* Power Query for data cleaning and transformation,
* DAX for calculations,

 Key Findings

1. Overall Revenue: The dashboard reports total revenue of approximately ₦73 billion, providing an overview of sales performance across the selected data.

2. Revenue by Market: Ekiti recorded the highest revenue among the five markets displayed, at approximately ₦5.6 billion, followed by Abia at ₦5.0 billion.

3. Revenue by Product Model: Model 3002P generated the highest displayed revenue, at approximately ₦26 billion, followed by model 4500C at approximately ₦17 billion.

4. Revenue by Store: Ankpa was the leading store among those displayed, generating approximately ₦1.71 billion in revenue.

5. Revenue by Day Category: Workdays accounted for the largest displayed revenue, at approximately ₦21 billion. Further analysis could help explain the difference between workdays and other day categories.

6. Interactive Analysis: The dashboard includes filters for Line of Business and Trade Date, allowing users to explore sales performance across different business lines and years.
